# Changelog

## Sites v30

- Require owner elevation for homework publication and study content administration, including fresh installs with no configured owner key.
- Keep ordinary reading and cloud saves available when owner tools are locked.

## Sites v29

- Replace owned treats or accessories atomically, with a preview of equipment changes.
- Add optional occasions and inclusive UTC grant cutoffs to collectible badges. Earned badges remain visible after the cutoff.

## Sites v28

- Include a collectible badge in a reusable package or one-time gift. A retired, stale or already-owned badge blocks the whole grant.
- Protect paid course reward history across ordinary cloud saves and checkpoint resume.

## Sites v27

- View owner-only study coverage, first-attempt accuracy, practice priorities, homework completion and world progress.
- Prevent the owner dashboard from being embedded in a frame.

## Sites v26

- Create, download and restore verified owner snapshots. Whole-account progress reset requires a current verified snapshot and exact username confirmation.
- Adapt runner drawing resolution under sustained slow frames without changing the visible viewport or game rules.

## Sites v25

- Replace pets atomically while preserving XP, equipment and compatible customization.
- Reset one saved stage score or one test and world best with a reviewed owner action.
- Activate runner controls using focused keyboard buttons.

## Sites v24

- Create and grant exclusive collectible badges through Game Master. Players see earned badges in Rewards.
- Download a private recovery QR PNG after rotating an explorer credential.
- Decode shop portraits as cards approach the viewport. Keep full companion art in the preview.

## Sites v23

- Reading Detective accepts optional keyword selections, separates scrolling from evidence taps, supports keyboard selection, and preserves additional verified evidence without paying twice.
- The Crystal Store shows paid previews for higher-tier pets and disables confirmation when the wallet is short. Runner controls clear stale swipe state when play loses focus.
- Game Master adds owner-only account management, crystal adjustments, pet and item grants, reward packages, targeted repairs, backups and audit history.
- New recovery credentials expire after 180 days and rotate after use. Older recovery codes retain their existing one-time use.
- GitHub CI builds from a clean install and runs unit, integration and touch-emulated browser tests.
